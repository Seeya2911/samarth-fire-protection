CASE LOG 01 • ALARM RECTIFICATION

F Residency Cooperative Housing Society

Location: Malad East, Mumbai • Building: 18-Storey Residential CHS
Client Name F Residency CHS
Property Type Residential Tower
Initial Status False Buzzer Alarms
Handover Status Form B Certified

1. Initial Requirement

The society's master alarm control panel was continuously triggering false emergency buzzer warnings. Several optical detector loops had ground-faulted behind corridor false ceilings, and the Public Address (PA) amplifier suffered from persistent electrical 50Hz hums during announcements.

2. Scope of Work & Execution

Instead of requiring the society to scrap and replace the entire panel system, our electronic technicians conducted megger loop isolation across all 18 floors. We isolated 2 shorted detector bases caused by monsoon moisture condensation, replaced corroded terminal plates, balanced ground impedance, and rewired the pre-amplifier input.

3. Verified Outcome & Handover

All 4 master loop zones returned to a quiet, normal operational baseline with zero false alarms over a 90-day monitoring window. The Public Address console broadcasts clear voice directives, and the building cleared its statutory Form B compliance inspection without defects.

Addressable Alarm Loop Zone Ground-Fault Isolation PA Hum Suppression Bi-Annual AMC Active
CASE LOG 02 • PUMP ROOM OVERHAUL
Vertical Wet Riser Welding

High-Rise Pump Station & Wet Riser Manifold Overhaul

Location: Western Suburbs, Mumbai • Building: 22-Storey Commercial & Retail Complex
Client Profile Commercial Block
System Scope 150mm Riser + Pumps
Initial Defect Riser Pressure Loss
Operating Result Stable at 7.5 Bar

1. Initial Requirement

A 22-storey commercial complex received a fire compliance notice after pressure in its vertical wet riser dropped below 2 bar. Underground pump check valves had seized open, causing continuous pump cycling and water backflow into raw storage tanks.

2. Scope of Work & Execution

Our mechanical team designed, fabricated, and installed a new carbon-steel pump manifold with dual cast-iron non-return valves, resilient-seated gate valves, and pressure relief bypasses. In the riser shaft, 18 corroded coupling gaskets were replaced with high-temperature EPDM grooved seals, and the jockey pump cut-in pressure switch was recalibrated.

3. Verified Outcome & Handover

The riser stack was hydro-tested to 10.5 kg/cm² for 2 hours with zero pressure drop. Roof landing valves now maintain a stable 3.5 kg/cm² running flow, fulfilling municipal fire safety standards. Handover documentation included certified Form A clearance and quarterly AMC scheduling.

150mm Main Manifold Dual Non-Return Valves 10.5 Bar Hydro-Tested Form A Cleared
CASE LOG 03 • COMMUNITY PREPAREDNESS
High-Pressure Hose Reel Drill

Housing Society Evacuation & CPR Mannequin Drill

Location: Kandivali West, Mumbai • Building: Residential CHS (140+ Apartments)
Target Group Residents & Guards
Attendance 70+ Participants
Components Fire + CPR Hands-On
Verification Drill Log Signed

1. Initial Requirement

The society's managing committee identified that while fire extinguishers were mounted in stairwells, over 90% of residents and security staff had never handled a pressurized cylinder or received instruction on emergency evacuation protocols.

2. Scope of Work & Execution

Our training instructors conducted a 3-hour comprehensive workshop: (1) Theory briefing on electrical and kitchen oil fires, (2) Live fire-pan suppression where every participant operated a 4kg ABC dry powder extinguisher, (3) 30-meter high-pressure hose reel water stream drill in the courtyard, and (4) Mannequin CPR chest compression practice.

3. Verified Outcome & Handover

Security guards demonstrated sub-90-second deployment of the courtyard yard hydrant hose line. Over 70 residents successfully completed live extinguishment and CPR compression checks. The society was issued an official fire drill completion record for municipal filing.

Live Fire-Pan PASS CPR Mannequin Coaching Security Guard Certification Drill Log Archived
DATA STANDARD & AUDIT TEMPLATE

Project Case-Study Documentation Standard

Every project log published by Samarth Fire Protection System must adhere to this verifiable reporting structure:

1. Project Title & Location: Specific building or municipal ward; client name disclosed only when authorized in writing.
2. Initial Deficiency / Notice: Concrete physical or regulatory issue (e.g. pressure loss, seized valve, continuous buzzer).
3. Engineering Scope & Action: Detailed components overhauled, pipe sizes, valves, and electrical loop interventions.
4. Outcome & Handover Docs: Measurable metric (bar pressure, loop stability) and statutory certificate (Form A / Form B).

Note: We uphold client privacy. In residential societies where written publicity release is pending, committee names are protected while preserving complete technical veracity.
