Frequently Asked Technical Questions

What is the functional difference between a Wet Riser and a Dry Riser?

A Wet Riser is permanently charged with water under constant pressure from an underground/overhead reservoir and automated booster pumps, ready for instantaneous water delivery during an emergency.

A Dry Riser remains empty under normal building conditions and is only pressurized when the municipal fire brigade arrives and pumps water into the external four-way breeching inlet at the building entrance.

What hydraulic pressure must a residential fire hydrant system maintain?

Under the National Building Code (NBC) 2016 Part 4 and Maharashtra Fire Prevention standards, high-rise wet riser networks must maintain the prescribed statutory running pressure at the topmost landing valve outlet while sustaining the designated hydrostatic test pressure across the riser column and pump manifold without leakage or pressure loss.

How frequently must fire fighting pumps and risers be inspected for Form B compliance?

Pumps should undergo routine weekly test runs to ensure starter panels, batteries, and diesel engines engage without delay. Complete physical servicing, gland repacking, valve lubrication, and statutory Form B certification must be completed and submitted twice yearly (in January and July) under Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act rules.
