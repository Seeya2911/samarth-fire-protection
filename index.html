THE EXPERTISE BEHIND THE ASSESSMENT

Sameer M. Kangutkar

Founder & Lead Consultant • Postgraduate, Disaster Management

Assessment-Led Philosophy: Sameer M. Kangutkar advocates that proper fire risk mitigation cannot be conducted remotely or from a catalog page. Unlike retail-focused equipment vendors who dispatch sales agents, Samarth Fire Protection leads with engineering inspections first.

Before proposing a specific system or servicing plan, we physically inspect building pressure lines, check exit stairwells for obstructions, and map electrical zone circuits. A proper technical audit is required to establish what a building actually needs to remain safe and compliant.

Personally oversees: Site Audits • Risk Identification • Project Planning • Clearance Oversight
