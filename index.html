AUTHENTIC OPERATIONAL METHODOLOGY

How We Prepare Communities: A 4-Step Reality

Most properties fail fire audits not because they lack equipment, but because valves are seized, alarms are disconnected, and occupants don't know how to act in the critical first two minutes.

STAGE 01

1. On-Site Physical Audit

Walkthrough inspection of risers, pump starters, refuge areas, and alarms alongside committee members to identify hidden safety gaps.

STAGE 02

2. Hardware Servicing

Gland packing lubrication, valve seat replacement, pressure gauge calibration, and non-return valve overhauls to ensure instant flow.

STAGE 03

3. Pump Synchronization

Automated starter testing, diesel engine fallback simulation, and jockey pressure switch tuning to prevent catastrophic pipe water hammer.

STAGE 04

4. Pressurized Live Drills

Full-pressure water discharge drill demonstrating how to manage branch recoil, aim streams, and suppress active structural heat.
