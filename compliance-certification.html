MUNICIPAL & TECHNICAL GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Form A and when is it issued?

Form A is a certificate issued by a licensed agency on the completion of the installation of fire prevention and life safety measures. It certifies that the installation has been executed in accordance with approved municipal drawings and conforms to fire safety standards.

Q: What is Form B and how often must a housing society file it?

Form B is a bi-annual clearance certificate. Under section 3(3) of the Maharashtra Fire Prevention Act, every owner or occupier of a building must submit a certificate from a licensed agency twice a year (every January and July) verifying that their fire safety installations are active and working.

Q: What is the penalty for not filing Form B certificates?

Failure to submit bi-annual Form B certificates is a violation of the Maharashtra Fire Act, which can result in the disconnection of utility services (power/water) to the building, prosecution, or cancellation of the occupancy clearance status.
