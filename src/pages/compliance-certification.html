COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Form A and Form B?

Form A is the initial certificate issued when a new fire prevention system is installed or commissioned, certifying that work conforms to approved plans. Form B is the recurring bi-annual certificate confirming that existing systems remain fully operational, pressurized, and maintained twice each year.

Who is authorized to issue a Form B certificate in Maharashtra?

Only a Licensed Agency holding a valid license issued by the Directorate of Maharashtra Fire Services (such as Samarth Fire Protection System, Licence No. MFS/LA/RF-34/RD-35) is legally authorized to inspect, sign, and issue Form B certificates under the Maharashtra Fire Act.

What happens if a building fails its initial Form B inspection?

If our engineers discover seized landing valves, disconnected alarm batteries, or discharged extinguishers during the audit, we issue an itemized defect notice. Once our crew rectifies the defective items, we execute the formal Form B clearance certificate without delay.
