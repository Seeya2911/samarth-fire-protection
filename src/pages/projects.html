ALARM RECTIFICATION

M/s. F Residency Cooperative Housing Society

Location: Malad, Mumbai
Ref: SFPS 2627 / 0006
Date: August 2026

The Problem

The society's master fire alarm panel was triggering continuous false buzzer warnings, causing confusion among residents. The public address (PA) console suffered from ground humming faults, and several detector loops had completely shut down due to short circuits.

The Challenge

Cabling was buried behind decorative false ceilings in lobby corridors. Identifying individual circuit failures without destroying plasterboards required zone isolation and signal continuity mapping at the terminal junctions.

The Solution

Samarth Fire Protection drafted a circuit override plan. Instead of proposing a costly system replacement, we isolated the shorted loops, traced the grounded conductors, replaced the faulty sensor nodes, and rebuilt the PA amplifier line connections.

The Execution

Our electronics and panel technicians systematically tested the zone terminals, isolated 4 faulty loop zones, replaced water-damaged bases in shafts, re-calibrated the main speaker pre-amp cards, and completed technical repairs under our Class D licensing parameters.

The Result

The fire alarm loop zone faults were cleared. The PA voice mic now broadcasts hum-free emergency calls. The system passed local safety checks, enabling the society to secure the required compliance parameters.
