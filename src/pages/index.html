AUTHENTIC OPERATIONAL METHODOLOGY

How We Prepare Communities: A 4-Step Reality

Most properties fail fire audits not because they lack equipment, but because valves are seized, alarms are disconnected, and occupants don't know how to act in the critical first two minutes.

STAGE 01

1. On-Site Audit & Briefing

Physical walkthrough of risers, pumps, refuge areas, and alarms alongside society committee members to identify hidden compliance gaps.

STAGE 02

2. Demystifying Equipment

Teaching residents, security staff, and housekeeping how to check pressure gauges, remove tamper seals, and execute the PASS technique safely.

STAGE 03

3. Hose Box & Branch Pipe Check

Inspecting internal cabinet nozzles, swing drum bearings, and rubber hose integrity to guarantee rapid first-response deployment.

STAGE 04

4. Pressurized Discharge Drills

Full-pressure water discharge drill demonstrating how to manage branch recoil, aim streams, and suppress external structural heat.
