A fire alarm panel can show an alarm, trouble or supervisory condition. These signals do not mean the same thing. If there is smoke, heat, a burning smell or an activated detector, treat the situation as a possible emergency and follow the building’s evacuation plan. Do not delay an evacuation to investigate the panel.

What information should the building team record?

From a safe location, note the exact message displayed, zone or device address, time, panel make/model if known, and whether the indication is continuous or intermittent. Inform the designated facility manager, security supervisor or fire-safety coordinator. Do not assume that one fault means every other part of the system is operating normally.

What should you avoid doing?

  • Do not switch off the panel simply to silence a buzzer.
  • Do not repeatedly reset the panel without understanding the reported condition.
  • Do not disconnect a detector, circuit, battery or sounder to make the message disappear.
  • Do not bypass a zone or disable a device unless the approved site procedure and authorised personnel require it.
  • Do not ask an untrained person to open the panel or change wiring.

Arrange competent fault rectification

Give the technician the panel message, affected zone or device, time of occurrence, recent power interruptions, renovation work and any previous fault history. Ask the service provider to identify the cause, work performed, parts replaced, remaining impairment and the tests used to verify the repair.

Confirm the system is restored

After rectification, check that the panel has returned to its normal condition, affected devices and circuits have been tested using the approved method, and temporary impairment measures have been closed by the responsible person. Update the fault register and inform the relevant building stakeholders.

What if a fire is suspected?

Do not troubleshoot during a suspected fire. Raise the alarm, follow the building’s emergency plan, evacuate where required and call the fire brigade / emergency services. Only attempt firefighting if you are trained, it is safe, the fire is small and you have a clear escape route.

Reference

Maintenance and testing should follow the installed system’s design, manufacturer instructions, applicable standards and directions of the competent authority. See the Maharashtra Fire & Emergency Services website for official information.
