COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does our fire alarm panel beep continuously with a trouble light?

A trouble light usually signals an open loop circuit, an end-of-line resistor failure, a disconnected smoke detector head, or a drained 24V backup battery pack. Our engineers trace the zone wiring with digital multimeters and restore healthy supervision without costly panel replacements.

Can addressable smoke detectors be added to an existing conventional system?

Conventional panels cannot address individual detectors by digital ID. However, we can integrate addressable zone interface modules or transition your property smoothly to a hybrid addressable master panel while utilizing existing fire-resistant wiring conduits.

How often should optical smoke detectors be cleaned?

In urban environments like Mumbai, construction dust and humidity accumulate in optical sensor chambers over time. IS 2189 mandates semi-annual detector head vacuuming and calibration to maintain optical sensitivity without triggering false alarm hooters.
