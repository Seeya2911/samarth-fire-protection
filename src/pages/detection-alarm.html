Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between conventional and addressable alarm panels?

Conventional panels divide a building into broad zones (e.g., Floor 3). You know smoke has been detected somewhere on that floor, but not which room. Addressable panels assign a unique ID to every single detector, showing the exact room or hallway location on an LCD screen, enabling much faster inspection.

Q: Why does our fire alarm panel show a continuous 'Fault' status light?

A 'Fault' light indicates an electrical issue rather than a fire. It is typically caused by a severed sensor wire, ground leakage, water intrusion in a detector base, or a failed backup battery. Resolving this requires zone isolation and continuity testing by a certified technician.

Q: Are smoke alarm loops mandatory for schools and nursing homes?

Yes. Under the National Building Code (NBC) guidelines and Maharashtra Fire Act, early smoke warnings are mandatory for educational and institutional buildings to facilitate timely evacuation of children and patients.
