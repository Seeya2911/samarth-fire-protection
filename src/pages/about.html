Company Overview

About Samarth Fire

Registered Class D Fire Agency • Malad East, Mumbai

We don't simply sell fire equipment. We assess what your property actually needs.

Founded on the principle of audit-led safety, Samarth Fire Protection System has established itself as an engineering-focused fire protection agency. Rather than functioning as a standard retail supply shop, we focus on identifying fire hazard gaps, designing compliant layouts, installing certified systems, and certifying emergency-ready buildings.

We operate under a valid license issued by the Directorate of Maharashtra Fire & Emergency Services (License No: MFS/LA/RF-34/RD-35), which permits us to install, commission, and certify Fire Fighting & Sprinkler systems, as well as Detection & Suppression systems. This license authorizes our team to issue Form A (on system completion) and Form B (six-monthly compliance checks), which are legally mandatory for buildings across Mumbai.

Our headquarters in Malad East coordinates engineers, firemen, pipe operators, and electrical specialists to inspect and service high-rise societies, educational institutes, industrial complexes, and commercial units.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

To provide assessment-led fire protection solutions that prioritize life safety and legal compliance. We serve Mumbai's housing societies and commercial assets by replacing generic equipment sales models with custom, site-specific safety engineering and transparent maintenance.

OUR DIRECTION

Our Vision

To set the benchmark for trade safety advisory in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR). We envision a future where high-rises and commercial hubs maintain continuous, functional fire readiness through regular, verified audits, proactive maintenance loops, and practical occupant training.

CORE VALUES

Values That Guide Our Safety Service

1. Integrity First

We never fabricate risk metrics or force societies into purchasing equipment they do not need. Transparent assessment coordinates everything we do.

2. Compliance Rigor

All installation, testing, and servicing follow guidelines set by the National Building Code (NBC) and the Maharashtra Fire Prevention Act.

3. Local Commitment

Based in Malad, we maintain rapid emergency troubleshooting support for active system breakdowns across the MMR.

4. Technical Clarity

We explain complicated codes and panel loops in plain, actionable language so housing society committees can make informed safety plans.

OPERATIONS CAPACITY

Engineering Capabilities

Our core technical expertise spans multiple building-service domains.

Suppression Engineering

Designing hydraulic wet riser networks, yard hydrants, foam suppression installations, kitchen chemical systems, and automatic sprinkler loops.

Includes: Booster pumps • Landing valves • Pipeline testing

Low-Voltage Systems

Wiring and commissioning analog/addressable fire alarm panels, configuring zone loops, smoke detection circuits, and public address speaker systems.

Includes: Loop troubleshooting • Sirens wiring • Beam detectors

Advisory & Compliance

Reviewing building drawings against NBC codes, conducting mock evacuation drills for high-rises, and providing support for CFO Fire NOC logs.

Includes: Form A & B • Evacuation plans • Staff training
