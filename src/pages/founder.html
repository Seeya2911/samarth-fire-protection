THE EXPERTISE BEHIND THE ASSESSMENT

Sameer M. Kangutkar

Postgraduate, Disaster Management

In Mumbai's highly dense housing societies and complex high-rises, fire safety cannot be solved with an equipment shopping list. It requires a deep understanding of risk planning, hazard identification, and government compliance requirements.

As a Postgraduate in Disaster Management, Sameer M. Kangutkar founded Samarth Fire Protection System to bridge the gap between regulatory mandates and actual mechanical safety. His academic and practical background allows him to evaluate building exits, pipe pressures, structural fire barriers, and alarm circuits as a unified safety network rather than isolated products.

Assessment-Led Philosophy: Sameer M. Kangutkar advocates that proper fire risk mitigation cannot be conducted remotely or from a catalog page. Unlike retail-focused equipment vendors who dispatch sales agents, Samarth Fire Protection leads with engineering inspections first. Before proposing any works, we physically inspect building pressure lines, check exit stairwells, and map electrical zone circuits to establish what a building actually needs to remain safe and compliant.

Sameer's hands-on management ensures that every installation project—whether a minor alarm repair or a complete high-rise hydrant overhaul—is executed to the standards required to pass Directorate of Maharashtra Fire Service inspections and issue legal Form A/B certifications.
