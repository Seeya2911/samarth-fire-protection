Quick answers

Fire Safety FAQs

Practical answers for building owners, housing society committees, facility managers and workplace teams. For legal or technical decisions, check the current official rules and the requirements for your specific property.

Read the Fire Safety Blog Maharashtra Fire Services circulars

These answers are general information, not a certificate or a substitute for a site inspection. Requirements depend on the approved fire plan, installed equipment, applicable standards and current authority directions.

Topic 1

Maharashtra fire forms and compliance

What is the difference between Form A and Form B?+
Form A is associated with the installation of fire prevention and life-safety measures. Form B relates to the maintenance of the applicable measures. They serve different purposes. Neither should be treated as a general replacement for a Fire NOC or other approval.
Is Form B the same as a Fire NOC?+
No. Form B is a maintenance certificate under the Maharashtra fire-safety framework. A Fire NOC or fire approval is a separate approval for the relevant stage and purpose.
When does a building need to submit Form B in Maharashtra?+
The Directorate’s 7 July 2026 Form B guideline confirms that Form B is issued twice a year, in January and July. A January Form B covers 1 July to 31 December of the previous year; a July Form B covers 1 January to 30 June of the current year. Check the current guideline and confirm local submission instructions.
What documents are required with Form B?+
The Directorate’s 7 July 2026 guideline lists a covering letter on the owner / occupier’s letterhead; applicable Form B appendices for installed systems; an AMC / CAMC contract or work-order confirmation showing the period, systems and appointed agency (without the amount); signed recent service / checking / test reports; the latest digitally signed site inspection report; and a valid Directorate licence copy. The digital signature on Form B must match the one on the licence. Confirm the current official template and any additional local requirements.
Who can issue a Form B certificate?+
Use a licensed agency with a current Directorate of Maharashtra Fire & Emergency Services licence covering the relevant systems and work. The Form B guideline requires the licence copy and says the digital signature on Form B should match the signature on the Director-issued licence.
Can a building split Form B maintenance among multiple agencies?+
The 7 July 2026 guideline says each major system group should be maintained by one agency for the entire building or occupancy: fire-fighting / water-based systems; detection, alarm and suppression systems; and passive measures such as fire doors, sealants, pressurisation and smoke management. Confirm the precise system grouping and scope with the Directorate.
Does a Form B certificate mean every fire safety issue is fixed?+
A certificate should reflect the checks and condition of the measures covered. It is not a guarantee that no fire can occur, and it does not remove the need to report faults, keep escape routes clear or maintain the systems between inspections.

Topic 2

Fire extinguishers and equipment

How often should fire extinguishers be inspected?+
IS 2190:2024 says extinguishers should be checked when first placed in service and then at approximately 90-day intervals, more frequently when circumstances require. The same standard specifies maintenance once a year, but not less than six months apart, and also when hydrostatic testing or an inspection indicates it is needed. Routine checks and full maintenance are not the same thing.
When should a fire extinguisher be refilled?+
A rechargeable extinguisher should be recharged after any use, even a brief discharge, or when an inspection or maintenance check identifies a deficiency. Use only the extinguishing medium specified by the manufacturer and on the extinguisher label.
What is hydrostatic pressure testing (HPT)?+
HPT checks whether an extinguisher cylinder can safely withstand the test pressure specified for its design. The test interval and method depend on extinguisher type and applicable requirements. Ask for the test result and an updated service tag or record.
Which extinguisher should be used near electrical equipment?+
The correct extinguisher depends on the equipment, whether it is energised and the extinguisher’s rating and label. Use only a type specifically suitable for the electrical hazard and never use water on live electrical equipment. Raise the alarm and evacuate if the fire is not small and safe to tackle.
Can one type of extinguisher be used for every fire?+
No. Water, foam, dry powder, CO₂, wet chemical and specialist agents are intended for different fire risks. The correct type and quantity should follow the site fire plan, the equipment label and competent advice.

Topic 3

Fire alarms, hydrants and maintenance

What should I do if the fire alarm panel shows a fault?+
Do not ignore the indication, switch off the panel or repeatedly reset it just to clear the display. Check for signs of an actual fire from a safe location, follow the building’s emergency procedure, record the message or affected zone and arrange a competent technician to investigate.
What is the difference between a conventional and an addressable fire alarm system?+
A conventional panel usually identifies the zone where an alarm or fault has occurred. An addressable panel can identify an individual device on its loop. The right system depends on the building layout, approved fire strategy and applicable requirements.
What is a wet riser or fire hydrant system?+
A wet riser is a vertical pipe kept charged with water so that an outlet is available on the floors it serves. A hydrant system can include pumps, water storage, pipework, landing valves, hose reels and other fittings. The exact arrangement depends on the building’s design.
What should a fire system AMC include?+
The scope should list the equipment covered, planned visits and tests, fault reporting, call-outs, replacement parts, service reports and how defects are closed. It may cover pumps, hydrants, sprinklers, alarms, extinguishers or other installed systems. The contract should reflect the equipment at the site.
How often should a fire alarm or hydrant system be serviced?+
There is no single interval that is correct for every building and every type of equipment. Follow the applicable standard, approved fire plan, manufacturer’s instructions, system design and competent authority’s directions. The AMC should make the agreed maintenance schedule clear.

Fire drills, training and services

What should a housing society fire drill include?+
A planned drill should cover the alarm, evacuation routes, wardens or responsible persons, assembly point, headcount and how to report missing people. It should follow the building’s approved emergency plan and include arrangements for residents who may need assistance.
