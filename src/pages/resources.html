Forms and Maharashtra compliance

What is the difference between Form A and Form B?

Form A is the licensed agency’s certificate regarding compliance of the fire-prevention and life-safety measures required in a building or part of it; it is commonly relevant to the installation or approval stage. Form B is the periodic certificate about maintenance of those measures during the preceding six months. They have different purposes. The building’s approved fire plan and current Maharashtra rules determine which systems and records apply.

See the Maharashtra Fire & Emergency Services Acts and Rules and the Directorate circulars.

Is Form B the same as a Fire NOC?

No. Form B is a periodic maintenance certificate under the Maharashtra fire-safety framework. A Fire NOC or fire approval is a separate approval or clearance for the relevant stage and purpose. Do not treat a Form B certificate as a replacement for a building’s required fire approval.

When is Form B due in Maharashtra?

The Directorate’s 7 July 2026 Guidelines to issue Form B describe the six-month reporting periods: the January Form B covers 1 July to 31 December of the preceding year; the July Form B covers 1 January to 30 June of the current year. Form B validity then runs until the next scheduled maintenance under the applicable Indian Standard and/or until the next Form B is issued, subject to the maintenance contract. The six-month filing period does not replace equipment-level maintenance intervals. Confirm the local submission route with the Chief Fire Officer or nominated officer.

Read the Directorate’s 7 July 2026 Form B guidelines (PDF), the official circular list and the Act and Rules.

What documents may be needed for a Form B submission?

The Directorate’s July 2026 guidance lists a minimum supporting set: a covering letter on the owner/occupier’s letterhead; the applicable Form B appendices for the installed systems; an AMC/CAMC order copy (without the price) or work-order confirmation stating the period, systems and agency; signed service and test reports for the relevant period; the latest site inspection report digitally signed by both parties; and a copy of the licensed agency’s valid licence. The digital signature on Form B must match the signature on the licence. The prescribed Form B wording must not be changed. The circular also says the listed documents are a minimum, not an exhaustive set.

Check the latest Maharashtra Fire Services circulars before filing.

What is Form B-1? Is it the same as Form B?

No. The Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures (Amendment) Rules, 2025 introduced Form A-1 for the certificate confirming installation of an Automated Continuous Monitoring System (ACMS), and Form B-1 for the related twice-yearly maintenance certificate for fire-fighting systems monitored through ACMS. Form A/B and Form A-1/B-1 serve different purposes; the ACMS forms apply where the relevant ACMS provisions apply to the building.

Read the 2025 Amendment Rules (PDF) and the official Acts and Rules page.

Who can issue the maintenance certificate?

Form B sits within the licensed-agency framework under Maharashtra fire-safety law. Check that the agency has a current Directorate licence covering the relevant systems, and make sure the prescribed form, annexures and digital-signature requirements are followed. A quotation, work order or ordinary service note is not a Form B certificate.

Fire extinguishers and equipment checks

How often should a portable fire extinguisher be checked?

IS 2190:2024 says extinguishers should be checked when first placed in service and thereafter at approximately 90-day intervals, with more frequent checks where circumstances require. Checks include location, access, visibility, legible instructions, seals, fullness, damage or leakage, the pressure indicator where provided, and the inspection and HPT tags. Follow the current standard and the equipment manufacturer’s instructions.

Reference: BIS IS 2190:2024.

How often does a fire extinguisher need maintenance?

IS 2190:2024 states that, except for the cases identified in its Annex D, extinguishers are to receive maintenance once a year and not less than six months apart, as well as at hydrostatic testing or when an inspection shows it is needed. The specific procedure depends on the extinguisher type. A visual check is not a substitute for maintenance by a competent service provider.

Reference: BIS standard details for IS 2190:2024.

What is HPT for a fire extinguisher?

HPT means hydrostatic pressure testing. It checks whether the extinguisher cylinder can safely withstand the required test pressure. The interval and procedure depend on the extinguisher category and the applicable standard. Do not apply one generic “every three” or “every five years” interval to every cylinder; check its type, markings, service history and the current IS 2190 requirements.

Can I use a water extinguisher on an electrical or oil fire?

Do not apply a water jet to energised electrical equipment or burning oil. It can cause electric shock or spread burning liquid. Raise the alarm, isolate power or fuel only if it is safe to do so, and use only an extinguisher suitable for the fuel and listed on its label. If the fire is growing or the escape route is not clear, evacuate and call emergency services.

Fire alarm, hydrant systems, audits and AMC

What should a building fire safety audit cover?

A building-specific review can cover the installed fire pumps, hydrants and wet risers, hose reels, sprinklers, extinguishers, alarm and detection systems, fire doors, exits, emergency lighting, signage, access routes, housekeeping and maintenance records. The scope depends on the building’s use, height, approved fire plans and applicable requirements. A generic checklist cannot confirm that a building is compliant.

Is there one pressure value for every fire hydrant system?

No. Required pressure and flow depend on the building, system design, hydraulic calculations, pump duty, outlet location, approved documents and applicable standards. Use the design and test criteria for that specific installation. Do not set or advertise a universal pressure value for every building.

What should a fire system AMC include?

An AMC should clearly state which systems and locations are covered, the inspection and test schedule, the work included, parts or consumables excluded, how faults are reported, response arrangements, service-report format and how defects are closed. Fire alarm, hydrant, pump, sprinkler and passive fire systems have different checks, so the contract should name the actual equipment rather than just say “complete fire AMC.”

What should we do when a fire alarm panel shows a fault?

Do not switch off, bypass or repeatedly reset a panel simply to silence the buzzer. Follow your building’s response plan, check for signs of an actual fire from a safe location, record the message or affected zone/device, inform the responsible person and arrange a competent technician. If there is smoke, heat or a suspected fire, activate the emergency response and evacuate as required.

What is the difference between a wet riser and a dry riser?

A wet riser is normally kept charged with water and connected to the building’s fire-water supply and pumps. A dry riser is normally empty and is filled by the fire brigade through its inlet when required. Which arrangement is required depends on the building design and applicable fire requirements.

Fire drills, first aid and service coverage

What should a housing society fire drill include?

A practical drill should follow the building’s approved emergency plan. It normally includes a coordinator and floor wardens, resident communication, safe exit routes, an assembly point, arrangements for people needing assistance, headcount and a written review of what should improve. Equipment demonstrations should be planned and supervised by competent trainers. The Directorate’s July 2026 Form B guidance also refers to periodic mock drills and supporting records.
