What is the difference between Form A and Form B?

Form A is associated with installation of fire-fighting / life-safety systems. Form B concerns periodic maintenance of the measures applicable to the building. They serve different purposes and neither should be described as a general-purpose Fire NOC.

Is Form B the same as a Fire NOC?

No. Form B is a maintenance certificate under the Maharashtra fire-safety framework. Fire approval / Fire NOC is a separate approval or clearance for the relevant stage and purpose.

When should a building submit Form B in Maharashtra?

The Directorate publishes current Form B guidance and circulars. Its circular index lists Guidelines to issue Form B dated 7 July 2026. An earlier clarification discusses the January and July six-monthly cycle. Confirm the current filing period, format and submission route with the local Chief Fire Officer or nominated officer.

Who can issue a Form B certificate?

The certificate is connected to the licensed-agency framework under Maharashtra fire-safety law. Verify that the agency has a valid Directorate licence covering the relevant work and follow the latest Directorate instructions. Do not accept an informal letter or an unverified copy as a substitute for the prescribed certificate.

What does a building fire safety audit include?

A building-specific review may cover installed fire pumps, hydrants, hose reels, extinguishers, alarm and detection systems, exits, fire doors, emergency lighting, signage, housekeeping and maintenance records. The final scope depends on the building, approved plans and applicable requirements.

Is there one pressure value for every fire hydrant system?

No. Required pressure and flow depend on building height, system design, hydraulic calculations, approved plans, applicable standards and authority requirements. Values must be verified against the particular system rather than copied from a generic online checklist.

Should a fire alarm panel be switched off when it shows a fault?

No. Do not switch off, bypass or repeatedly reset a fire alarm panel to hide a fault. Follow the building's response procedure, record the indicated zone or device, notify the responsible person and arrange repair by a competent technician. Treat signs of fire as an emergency.

How often should fire extinguishers be serviced?

The correct inspection, maintenance, refilling and hydrostatic-test requirements depend on extinguisher type, manufacturer instructions, applicable standards and service condition. Use a competent service provider and do not apply one generic interval to every extinguisher.

Can a housing society conduct a fire drill without professional support?

Societies should follow their approved emergency plan and applicable local requirements. A drill needs a coordinator, safe routes, an assembly point, resident communication and assistance arrangements. Practical equipment demonstrations should be risk-assessed and supervised by competent trainers.

Which areas does Samarth Fire Protection System serve?

The website describes service coverage across Mumbai and the wider Mumbai Metropolitan Region, including Malad, Goregaon, Kandivali, Borivali, Bandra, Thane and Navi Mumbai. Confirm availability for the specific site before scheduling.

Check the official Maharashtra guidance

The Directorate’s website publishes the Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act, amended rules and circulars. Its circular index lists Guidelines to issue Form B dated 7 July 2026. Use the latest official publication rather than relying only on older summaries.
