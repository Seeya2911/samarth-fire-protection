Fire Alarm Panel Faults: Safe Response for Building Managers

A fault indication on a fire alarm panel is a maintenance issue that needs prompt attention. It is not a reason to ignore the panel, silence a recurring problem indefinitely or leave the building without a functioning warning system.

Distinguish an alarm from a fault

Follow the site's emergency procedure whenever the panel indicates an alarm or occupants report signs of fire. A trouble or fault signal may indicate a wiring issue, power problem, device fault, communication loss or another abnormal condition. Staff should know the difference and know whom to contact.

Record the panel information

Note the exact text, zone, loop or device reference shown on the panel, along with the time and any recent building work. Photograph the display if permitted. This information helps the service engineer trace the fault without repeated resets that erase useful clues.

Do not disable protection as a workaround

Do not disconnect detectors, bypass zones, remove fuses or leave the panel powered down to stop nuisance indications. Any necessary isolation must be authorised, limited to the required scope and managed under the building's impairment procedure with compensatory precautions.

Call a competent service professional

Arrange diagnosis by a competent fire-alarm technician familiar with the installed make and system configuration. Ask for the cause, affected devices or zones, repair completed, tests performed and any remaining limitations to be recorded in the service report.

Communicate with occupants and security

Tell the responsible building team what area is affected and what temporary arrangements apply. Ensure security and facility staff know how to raise an alarm manually and who to call. Follow the building's emergency plan and authority requirements while protection is impaired.

Verify restoration

After repair, confirm that the panel returns to its normal condition and that the affected devices and functions have been tested. Obtain written confirmation of the work and close the impairment log only when the system is confirmed restored.

Frequently asked questions

Can we keep resetting a recurring fault?

Repeated resets do not fix the underlying cause. Record the fault and arrange diagnosis.

Should the fire alarm panel be switched off during maintenance?

Only when necessary, authorised and controlled under the site's impairment procedure. Maintain compensatory precautions and restore the system promptly.

Who should repair the system?

A competent technician familiar with the installed fire alarm system and applicable requirements.

Need a site-specific fire safety assessment?

Requirements depend on the building, installed systems and applicable rules. Speak with the team about an assessment or maintenance visit.
