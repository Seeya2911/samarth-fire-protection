Privacy Policy

Samarth Fire Protection System • Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who We Are

Samarth Fire Protection System (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website samarth-fire-protection.vercel.app. We are a Government of Maharashtra licensed Class D fire protection agency (Licence No. MFS/LA/RF-34/RD-35), registered at C/611, Parekh Nagar, CTS 710, Pathan Wadi, Malad (East), Mumbai – 400097, Maharashtra, India.

Contact us: samarthfireprotectionsystem@gmail.com • +91 9967101472

2. What Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide when you use our contact, quotation, audit-booking or fault-report forms:

  • Name (required)
  • Mobile number (required, 10-digit Indian mobile)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Organisation / Building name (optional)
  • Service requirement, property details, message (optional, as entered by you)
  • Attachments — PDF, JPG or PNG up to 1.5 MB, submitted voluntarily for site-visit or fault-report requests

We do not use tracking cookies, browser fingerprinting, advertising networks, or any third-party analytics on this website. We do not collect payment card data.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your enquiry, quotation request, or service booking
  • Coordinate site visits, audits, or maintenance schedules as requested
  • Send you service-related communications (not marketing emails)

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

4. How Your Information Reaches Us

Form submissions are sent to our inbox via Resend (resend.com), a transactional email service. Resend acts as a data processor on our behalf and receives only the form content required to deliver the email. Resend’s servers are located in the United States; by submitting a form you consent to this transfer for the purpose of delivering your enquiry to us.

No form data is stored in a database on our website. The email delivered to our inbox is retained in our Gmail mailbox in accordance with Section 5 below.

5. Data Retention

Enquiry emails are retained in our inbox for up to 3 years for reference and to handle any warranty or service-history queries. If you would like your data deleted earlier, please email us at samarthfireprotectionsystem@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023 & IT Act 2000)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and the Information Technology Act, 2000, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of any further communication from us

To exercise any of these rights, email samarthfireprotectionsystem@gmail.com with “Data Request” in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are directed at adults (business and property owners, facility managers). We do not knowingly collect personal data from persons under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our website, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. External Links

This website contains links to external sites (e.g., Google Maps, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

9. Security

We use HTTPS (TLS) for all data in transit. Our hosting platform (Vercel) applies industry-standard server security. No internet transmission is 100% secure; please do not send sensitive documents through the web form unless necessary.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
