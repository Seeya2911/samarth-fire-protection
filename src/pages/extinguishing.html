TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

Cylinder Engineering Specifications

Every refilled extinguisher undergoes nitrogen pressurization, scale weighing, and leak immersion testing before site delivery.

01

ABC Dry Chemical Powder

Monoammonium Phosphate (MAP 50% / 90%) pressurized with dry nitrogen gas. Multi-purpose suppression for Class A (wood), Class B (fuels), and Class C (electrical).

02

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) Gas

Seamless manganese steel or carbon-steel cylinders filled with liquid CO₂ gas for server rooms, elevator shafts, electrical panels, and clean laboratories.

03

Mechanical Foam (AFFF)

Aqueous Film Forming Foam creates an airtight blanket over diesel generator rooms, solvent tanks, and parking oil leaks, cutting oxygen supply instantly.

04

Hydraulic Pressure Test (HPT)

Mandatory shell expansion testing at 30 to 250 kg/cm² to detect micro-cracks and seam fatigue before recharging cylinders with working pressures.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How can we verify if our building fire extinguishers need refilling?

Check the dial pressure gauge on the cylinder valve: if the needle rests in the red discharge zone or if the statutory inspection tag is older than 12 months, immediate refilling and re-pressurization are required under IS 2190 regulations.

Do you provide standby cylinders during refilling pickup?

Yes. To ensure your housing society or commercial facility is never left vulnerable or unprotected while cylinders undergo hydraulic testing at our workshop, we provide standby replacement extinguishers.

What is the difference between ABC Powder and CO₂ extinguishers?

ABC powder smothers solid combustibles, liquid fires, and electrical components with a fine non-conductive chemical layer. CO₂ gas displaces oxygen and cools electrical components without leaving chemical residues, making it ideal for servers and high-value equipment.
