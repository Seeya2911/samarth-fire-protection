MUNICIPAL & TECHNICAL GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Hydrostatic Pressure Testing (HPT) and is it mandatory?

Yes, HPT is legally mandatory under Indian Standards (IS 2190). Cylinders must undergo hydrostatic pressure testing at specified intervals (usually every 3 to 5 years depending on the type) to verify that the cylinder shell can handle the intense internal pressure of the gas charge.

Q: How often do fire extinguishers need refilling in India?

Fire extinguishers must be recharged immediately after any discharge, even a partial one. If unused, dry powder and mechanical foam extinguishers require refilling and chemical replacement periodically (typically every 1 to 3 years) to prevent chemical clumping, while CO₂ cylinders are checked by weight annually.

Q: What type of extinguisher is best for electrical control panels?

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) and Clean Agent (such as FM200 or HFC gases) extinguishers are best because they are non-conductive, leave no chemical residue, and won't damage sensitive electronics, unlike Dry Chemical Powder (DCP) which leaves corrosive residues.
