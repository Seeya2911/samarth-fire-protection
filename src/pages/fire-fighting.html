COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Wet Riser and a Dry Riser?

A Wet Riser is permanently charged with water under pressure from terrace tanks and booster pumps, ready for instantaneous operation during an emergency. A Dry Riser remains empty during normal conditions and is pressurized by fire brigade pump engines connecting to external inlet breeching valves at ground level.

What pressure must a residential fire hydrant system maintain?

Under National Building Code (NBC) 2016 Part 4 and Maharashtra Fire Prevention standards, high-rise risers must maintain a minimum running pressure of 3.5 kg/cm² (bar) at the topmost hydrant outlet, while withstanding a test pressure of 10 kg/cm² at the basement pump manifold without leakage.

How frequently must fire fighting pumps and hydrants be serviced?

Pumps should undergo automated weekly test-runs to ensure battery starters and diesel fallback engines engage smoothly. Full physical servicing, gland packing inspection, and statutory bi-annual Form B certification must be filed with the municipal fire department twice yearly (January and July).
