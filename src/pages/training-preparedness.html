Operational Life Safety

Training, Mock Drills & CPR Workshops

Hands-On Extinguisher Drills • CPR Resuscitation • Residential Safety Walks

The Real-World Principle: A housing society or commercial plant can possess high-end sprinklers and fire hydrants, but if residents, housekeeping, or security guards hesitate during the critical initial two minutes, catastrophe strikes. Samarth conducts hands-on, certified mock drills and first-aid/CPR training across Mumbai.

Real Equipment. Real Practice. Zero Hesitation.

Our training isn't a passive slide presentation. We assemble residents and staff in open courtyards, light controlled tray fires, and place fire extinguishers directly into their hands to build muscle memory.

Live Fire Simulation

Controlled Fire Discharges

Residents practice pulling safety pins, aiming nozzles at the base of flame, and sweeping smoothly to extinguish active fires.

High-Pressure Operations

Hose Reel Deployment Drills

Practical training on unreeling 30-meter high-pressure hose drums, controlling water branch nozzles, and operating landing valves under pressure.

Community Inclusion

Demystifying Safety for All

Dedicated sessions for homemakers, seniors, and domestic staff on domestic gas cylinder leaks, electrical breaker trips, and stairwell safety.

1. Evacuation Drills

Simulated building alarm triggers, designated assembly area rendezvous, smoke avoidance postures, and staircase crowd control.

2. Cylinder Operation

Difference between Class A, B, and C fires, correct chemical agent selection (Powder vs. CO2), and pressure gauge reading.

3. Hydrant Hose Running

Coupling canvas hoses, opening landing valves, managing nozzle water kickback, and operating swing-type hose reel drums.

4. Staff Certification

Formal attendance documentation and basic emergency responder verification for security agencies and facility staff.
