Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How often should a housing society conduct a fire mock drill?

Housing societies should conduct at least one fire safety drill annually. This ensures that security personnel, housekeeping staff, and residents are familiar with evacuation routes, assembly areas, and basic extinguisher operations.

Q: What is covered in a standard fire safety training session?

A standard session includes hazard identification checks, evacuation route mapping, an overview of active fire systems (sprinklers/detectors), and live practice operating different types of fire extinguishers on minor controlled fires.

Q: What is the PASS method for using a fire extinguisher?

PASS stands for: Pull the safety pin; Aim the nozzle at the base of the fire; Squeeze the operating lever; Sweep the nozzle from side to side across the base of the fire.
