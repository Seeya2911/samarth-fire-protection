Field Practice Drill

Technician-led pressurized hose reel discharge and nozzle control training for society security and housekeeping staff.

The Life Safety Principle: A housing society or commercial tower can possess high-capacity sprinklers and risers, but if occupants or security personnel hesitate during the first two minutes, catastrophe strikes. Samarth conducts hands-on, practical mock drills, controlled extinguisher discharges, and life-saving first-aid workshops across Mumbai.

TRAINING CURRICULUM

Structured Practical Modules

Our training is not an abstract slide presentation. We assemble residents and staff in open compounds, explain equipment physics, and provide direct hands-on practice.

01

Evacuation Drills

Simulated building alarm triggers, designated assembly area rendezvous, smoke avoidance crawl postures, and stairwell crowd management.

02

Extinguisher Operation

Understanding Class A, B, and C fires, correct chemical selection (ABC Dry Powder vs. CO₂ gas), and the PASS operational protocol (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep).

03

Hydrant & Hose Running

Coupling canvas hoses, opening landing valves, managing nozzle water pressure kickback, and operating swing-type hose reel drums effectively.

04

Staff & Guard Certification

Formal attendance documentation, emergency contact protocol checklists, and certificate issuance for security agencies and facility management staff.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can drills be conducted on Sunday mornings for housing societies?

Yes. The majority of our residential housing society drills are conducted on Sunday mornings (typically 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM) when maximum residents, committee members, and domestic staff are present.

Do you provide training certificates for commercial audit compliance?

Yes. We issue formal training participation certificates and attendance rosters that fulfill fire audit, factory inspection, and municipal compliance requirements.

Is the CPR training included in the standard mock drill?

We offer both Fire-Only practical drills and comprehensive Fire + CPR/First-Aid workshops. During CPR sessions, certified instructors demonstrate chest compression rhythms on medical training mannequins.
