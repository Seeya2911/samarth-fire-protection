Passive Fire Protection

Compartmentation • Fire Rated Doors (FRD) • Cable Seals

Definition: Passive fire protection refers to structural safety components designed to contain fire, heat, and toxic smoke within localized building compartments using firedoors, structural barriers, electrical shaft fire-stops, and fire-resistant materials. Samarth Fire Protection System installs certified passive barriers to prevent structural failures and block smoke paths in Mumbai high-rises.

Compartmentation Specifications

While active systems extinguish fires, passive systems contain them. We secure electrical and mechanical pathways to block smoke chimney effects.

Fire Rated Doors (FRD) Certified wooden or steel door assemblies rated for 60 to 120 minutes of heat resistance, complete with auto-closers and smoke seals.
Shaft Fire-Stopping Sealing open gaps in vertical electrical and communication cabling ducts using fire-retardant mortars and intumescent pillows.
Duct Fire Dampers Mechanical dampers installed in HVAC ducts that close automatically via thermal links to block hot gas pathways.
Intumescent Coatings Specialized paint coatings applied to load-bearing structural steel that expand under heat to form an insulating carbon layer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between active and passive fire protection?

Active fire protection systems require an action or trigger to operate (e.g. smoke triggering alarms, heat busting sprinklers, or operating fire extinguishers). Passive fire protection is structural and works without activation (e.g. fire doors containment, shaft mortar sealing, and fireproofing paints that delay fire spread).

Q: Why are electrical shafts considered high-risk areas in high-rises?

Electrical shafts run vertically through all floors of a building. If the gaps around the cables on each floor are not sealed with fire-resistant barrier materials, the shaft acts as a chimney. A fire in the basement will suck air and spread smoke and flames to the top floor within minutes.

Q: What are fire door ratings?

Fire door assemblies are tested and certified by laboratories to withstand heat, fire, and smoke penetration for specific durations. Common rating durations are 60 minutes, 90 minutes, and 120 minutes (1 or 2 hours), giving occupants time to evacuate safely.
