Our Methodology

The 7-Step Fire Safety Journey

Assess • Identify • Recommend • Quote • Execute • Test • Maintain

01

Assess

The safety lifecycle always begins on site. Sameer M. Kangutkar conducts a physical walkthrough of the premises, reviewing structural escape passages, wet riser paths, booster pump stations, panel boards, and current extinguisher placements.

Focus: Understand the property configuration
02

Identify

We identify system faults, broken components, leaks in hydrant lines, and shorted alarm zones. We cross-reference our observations with the Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act and the National Building Code (NBC) to list critical compliance violations.

Focus: Document requirements and deficiencies
03

Recommend

We define the appropriate fire suppression configuration, alarm panels, cabling gauges, and structural compartmentation details needed to bring the property into compliance. We present these recommendations in a structured review report.

Focus: Define the appropriate solution
04

Quote

Samarth Fire Protection drafts a transparent, itemized quotation listing specific quantities, material standards, and labor costs. We present cost items clearly without hidden handling fees.

Focus: Prepare the project quotation
05

Execute

Our team of technicians installs pipes, brackets, booster pumps, sprinkler networks, smoke alarms, and passive fire doors. Sameer M. Kangutkar personally monitors execution quality on-site.

Focus: Carry out approved works
06

Test

We pressure-test pipes up to operating parameters, verify alarm loop resistance hum-free, trigger smoke sensors, and check that booster pumps start on grid cutouts.

Focus: Verify system performance
07

Maintain

We establish annual maintenance routines (AMC) covering visual inspection, pump check runs, valve lubrication, and extinguisher servicing to keep safety systems functional.

Focus: Support ongoing fire safety
