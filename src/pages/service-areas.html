Mumbai and MMR

Local site services

Fire alarm and fire fighting installation, maintenance and AMC, audits, extinguisher work and training can be discussed for sites across Mumbai and the wider Mumbai Metropolitan Region. The team confirms availability and scope for each property.

Pan-India enquiries

Fire extinguisher supply, installation and maintenance

We welcome enquiries from cities, towns and rural areas across India. Delivery, travel, installation work and future servicing arrangements are confirmed for the location and project before an order is accepted.

Locations we welcome enquiries from

Along with Mumbai and the MMR, enquiries are welcome from other parts of Maharashtra and neighbouring states. Examples include Satara, Jalna, Gadchiroli and rural Maharashtra, as well as Karnataka. This is not a closed list—tell us the pin code and the type of work required.

What to share when you enquire

  • Your city, town or village and PIN code.
  • Property type: home, housing society, shop, office, factory, warehouse or institution.
  • Number and type of extinguishers, or the system that needs attention.
  • Whether you need new supply, installation, refilling, inspection, HPT or ongoing maintenance.
  • Any delivery or site constraints that the team should know about.

How we confirm a Pan-India request

We review the location, quantities, equipment type and the work requested. The team then confirms delivery, installation, travel or servicing arrangements and any applicable charges before the work is accepted. Do not assume that all on-site services are automatically available in every district; ask us to confirm your location.
