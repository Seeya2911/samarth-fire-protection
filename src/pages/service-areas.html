Our Mumbai base

From our Malad East office, we arrange site visits, installations, maintenance and fire safety support across Mumbai and the wider Mumbai Metropolitan Region, subject to the project requirements and schedule.

Pan-India extinguisher services

We welcome enquiries from across India for fire extinguisher supply, installation and maintenance. This includes cities, smaller towns and rural areas. Locations you may enquire from include Satara, Jalna, Gadchiroli and Karnataka, along with other parts of Maharashtra and the country.

For each enquiry, we confirm the equipment required, delivery or travel arrangements, installation scope and maintenance schedule before accepting the work. On-site coverage and timing depend on the location and project.
