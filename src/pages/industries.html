Oil, gas & petroleum

Fuel & Energy Facilities

Fire fighting equipment, extinguishers, alarm systems, maintenance and site support for fuel storage, processing and related facilities.

Typical needs: portable extinguishers, hydrants, pumps, detection, suppression and maintenance records.

Shopping malls & retail

Malls, Stores & Retail Properties

Fire alarm and fire fighting systems, extinguisher supply and upkeep, safety checks and staff awareness for public-facing spaces.

Typical needs: alarm checks, hydrants, sprinklers, exit routes, extinguishers and staff training.

Manufacturing & logistics

Factories, Plants & Warehouses

Supply, installation, testing, maintenance and upgrading of fire protection systems for industrial workplaces and storage areas.

Typical needs: hydrant lines, sprinklers, fire pumps, alarms, suppression, extinguishers and safety signs.

Residential properties

Housing Societies & High-Rise Buildings

Fire system maintenance, extinguisher servicing, site reviews, Form A / Form B support and practical fire drills for residents and security teams.

Typical needs: pump rooms, wet risers, hose reels, alarms, extinguishers, fire doors and evacuation awareness.

Healthcare & education

Hospitals, Schools & Institutions

Fire equipment, maintenance support and training for staff, students, patients, visitors and facility teams.

Typical needs: alarms, clear escape routes, extinguishers, staff instruction, first aid and CPR awareness.

Workplaces & public facilities

Offices, Government & Public Buildings

Fire safety system installation and servicing, equipment supply, safety reviews, demonstrations and training for teams and visitors.

Typical needs: alarm systems, extinguishers, fire fighting equipment, safety signs and emergency awareness.
