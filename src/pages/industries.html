Our work covers fire alarm and fire fighting systems, equipment supply, maintenance, safety checks and staff training. The right service depends on the site, the systems already installed and the work required.

Oil, Gas & Petroleum

Fire alarm and fire fighting equipment, extinguisher supply and maintenance for fuel, storage, processing and related facilities. We welcome enquiries from petroleum and energy organisations, including facilities operated by companies such as HPCL and BPCL.

Shopping Malls & Retail

Fire alarm systems, hydrants, sprinklers, extinguisher maintenance, audits and staff training for malls, shops and large retail properties, including properties such as Phoenix Marketcity, R City and Inorbit.

Factories, Industrial Sites & Warehouses

Fire fighting systems, alarm systems, extinguishers, passive fire protection, equipment maintenance and fire safety training for industrial workplaces.

Offices & Commercial Buildings

Installation and maintenance of alarm and fire fighting systems, extinguisher services, site checks and evacuation training for offices and business parks.

Hospitals, Schools & Institutions

Fire safety equipment and training for staff, students, visitors and facility teams in healthcare and education settings.

Housing Societies & Residential Buildings

Fire alarm and hydrant maintenance, extinguisher servicing, site audits, fire drills and guidance for society committees and building managers.

Note: HPCL, BPCL, Phoenix Marketcity, R City and Inorbit are examples of the sectors and properties Samarth Fire wants to serve. They are not presented here as confirmed customers or endorsements.
