01 · Petroleum & energy

Oil, Gas & Petroleum

Fire protection equipment, extinguisher servicing, site checks and safety training for fuel, storage and related facilities. Sector examples include HPCL and BPCL; these are not claims of current customer status.

02 · Retail & public spaces

Shopping Malls & Retail

Fire alarm and sprinkler maintenance, hydrants, extinguishers, emergency-access reviews and staff awareness for malls and retail properties.

03 · Industrial operations

Factories & Warehouses

Fire pumps, hydrants, hose systems, alarms, suppression equipment, passive protection, maintenance and workforce training.

04 · Commercial property

Offices & Business Parks

Alarm and fire-fighting installation, AMC, extinguisher servicing, audits and evacuation awareness for corporate premises.

05 · Public & institutional

Hospitals & Institutions

Fire equipment, maintenance records, evacuation planning and staff awareness for healthcare, schools and public-facing institutions.

06 · Residential property

Housing Societies & Towers

Hydrant and alarm maintenance, extinguisher checks, audits, resident demonstrations, mock drills and support for society committees.
