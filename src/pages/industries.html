Property types

Support shaped around your industry

These are the types of properties and organisations the company works with or wants to support. Exact scope is agreed after the location, equipment and service requirement are understood.

01 · Petroleum and energy

Oil, Gas & Petroleum Facilities

Fire fighting and alarm equipment, extinguisher supply and maintenance, site checks and safety training for fuel, storage and related facilities. Examples of the sector include large operators such as HPCL and BPCL; these names are not a claim of current customer status.

02 · Retail and public spaces

Shopping Malls & Retail

Fire alarm and sprinkler maintenance, hydrants, extinguishers, emergency access reviews and staff awareness for malls and retail properties such as Phoenix Marketcity, R City and Inorbit. These are examples of the market segment, not claimed clients.

03 · Industrial operations

Factories, Warehouses & Manufacturing

Fire pumps, hydrants, hose systems, alarms, suppression equipment, extinguishers, passive protection, maintenance and worker training for production and storage sites.

04 · Commercial property

Offices & Business Parks

Alarm and fire fighting system installation, AMC, extinguisher servicing, audits and evacuation awareness for office buildings, business parks and corporate premises.

05 · Public and institutional

Hospitals, Schools & Institutions

Fire alarm and fire fighting equipment, maintenance records, evacuation planning and staff awareness for healthcare, schools and other public-facing institutions.

06 · Residential property

Housing Societies & Residential Towers

Hydrant and alarm maintenance, extinguisher checks, fire safety audits, resident demonstrations, mock drills and support for society committees and facility managers.
