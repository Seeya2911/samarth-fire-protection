Firefighting Systems

FIELD DOCUMENTATION Cylinder Audit & Recharging
03

Fire Extinguishers

What it covers: Supply of new ISI-marked ABC Dry Powder (MAP 50%/90%), CO₂ gas, Mechanical Foam (AFFF), and Clean Agent cylinders. Periodic hydraulic pressure testing (HPT), chemical refilling, tamper-seal replacement, and statutory inspection tagging.

Who May Need It: All premises under IS 2190 requiring mandatory annual refilling and 3-to-5 year hydrostatic shell integrity checks.
What The Process Involves: Discharge testing, cylinder shell hydraulic expansion test, nitrogen pressurization, weighing, and verification tagging.
Quotation requirements: Quantity of cylinders, capacity (e.g. 4kg, 6kg, 9kg), extinguishing agent type, and last hydro-test year.
