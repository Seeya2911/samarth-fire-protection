Equipment & materials

Fire safety products we work with

The catalogue includes the following equipment and materials. The correct selection depends on the building, the hazard and the approved system design.

Water-based systems

Fire hydrants, hydrant-line products, hose and hose fittings, branches, nozzles, sprinklers and accessories.

Alarm & detection

Automatic fire alarm systems, alarm panels and different types of fire detectors.

Extinguishing & suppression

Fire extinguishers and suppression equipment using suitable water, foam, dry powder (DCP), gas-based and other approved media; breathing apparatus sets; foam equipment and accessories.

Passive protection

Fire barriers, FRP/FRD products, fire-rated doors and associated materials.

Safety equipment

Industrial safety equipment and safety signs, including road construction signs.

Testing & servicing

Inspection, testing, commissioning, servicing, HPT of extinguishers and upgrading of existing fire systems.

Training & demonstrations

Equipment-use demonstrations, fire drills, fire safety training and first aid / CPR training.

Compliance support

Form A and Form B documentation support, subject to the applicable rules, licence scope and Directorate instructions.
