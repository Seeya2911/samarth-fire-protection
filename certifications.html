Government Licensing & Compliance

Directorate of Maharashtra Fire & Emergency Services

Why Government Licensing Matters

Under the Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act, 2006, fire fighting installations, alarm loops, and suppression systems can only be executed, repaired, or certified by agencies licensed by the state government.

Any fire safety work done by unlicensed contractors, retailers, or electrical installers is considered non-compliant. Such works are legally invalid and cannot be used to obtain provisional or final fire clearances or to file Form B clearances.

AUTHORIZED COMPLIANCE CAPABILITIES

• Form A Authorization: Issued on completion of new system installations. It verifies that the equipment matches municipal rules and allows the society to obtain a Fire NOC.
• Form B Authorization: Issued twice a year (every January and July) following a physical safety check. This certifies that existing systems remain fully operational.

Samarth Fire Protection System maintains license MFS/LA/RF-34/RD-35 (Class D) in active, verified status. This ensures that every audit we conduct and every certificate we sign carries official authority.
