**Core Purpose:** Align commercial and informational search intent with dedicated, high-authority pages to eliminate internal keyword cannibalization and maximize qualified conversion.

---

## 1. Intent Clustering & Architecture

We divide user search intent into four primary tiers:
1. **Navigational / Brand:** Users seeking Samarth Fire, founder details, or licence credentials.
2. **Commercial Investigation:** Facility managers, CHS secretaries, and safety officers comparing contractors for fire hydrants, alarms, extinguishers, or AMCs.
3. **Transactional / Conversion:** Immediate enquiry submission, cost estimation, Form B compliance filing, or emergency fault rectification.
4. **Informational / Regulatory:** Queries on Maharashtra Fire Act compliance, IS codes, extinguisher refilling intervals, and evacuation drill requirements.

## 3. Internal Linking Architecture Rules

1. **Hierarchy First:** All interior pages maintain breadcrumb navigation pointing back to parent directories (`Home > Services > Discipline`).
2. **Contextual In-Text Links:** Content narratives reference related service hubs naturally (e.g., hydrant pages link to Form B compliance certification and AMC).
3. **Clear Conversion Pathway:** Every commercial and technical page ends with a high-contrast quotation callout directing users to `quote.html` or direct WhatsApp engagement.
4. **Anchor Text Integrity:** Avoid generic "click here" anchors; use descriptive, topical anchors such as *"Schedule a Form B Life-Safety Assessment"* or *"Explore Fire Hydrant & Pump Services"*.
