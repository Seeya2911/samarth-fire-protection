WHAT WE OFFER

Practical On-Site Fire Safety & CPR Training

We provide interactive, compound-level training sessions where your people physically handle firefighting equipment and master emergency response protocols.

01

Hands-On Extinguisher Drills

We bring training cylinders to your building compound, teach fire classification (Class A, B, C), and give every resident and guard direct, hands-on practice executing the PASS technique (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep).

02

Building Evacuation & Mock Drills

We coordinate full-scale building evacuation exercises, simulating alarm activation, demonstrating smoke-crawl postures, clearing stairways, and verifying headcounts at designated open-air assembly points.

03

Hydrant & Hose Reel Training

We train security guards and facility operators to unreel swing-type hose drums, couple canvas hoses to landing valves, open water supplies, and manage high-pressure branch nozzle kickback safely.

04

First Aid, CPR & Certification

We teach life-saving cardio-pulmonary resuscitation (CPR), AED awareness, burn management, and bleeding control, issuing formal certificates of participation and compliance documentation for society audits.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can drills be conducted on Sunday mornings for housing societies?

Yes. The majority of our residential housing society drills are conducted on Sunday mornings (typically 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM) when maximum residents, committee members, and domestic staff are present.

Do you provide training certificates for commercial audit compliance?

Yes. We issue formal training participation certificates and attendance rosters that fulfill fire audit, factory inspection, and municipal compliance requirements.

Is the CPR training included in the standard mock drill?

We offer both Fire-Only practical drills and comprehensive Fire + CPR/First-Aid workshops. During CPR sessions, certified instructors demonstrate chest compression rhythms on medical training mannequins.
