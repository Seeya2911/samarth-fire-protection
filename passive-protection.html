COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are fire doors required on residential staircase landings?

Stairways are the primary emergency escape route for occupants during a building fire. Under NBC 2016, 120-minute rated fire doors with self-closers ensure that smoke and toxic heat from a burning corridor cannot enter the staircase enclosure while residents evacuate.

Can fire doors be wedged open for fresh air ventilation?

No. Propping fire doors open is a severe municipal code violation. A wedged-open door permits lethal smoke to suffocate the entire staircase within 90 seconds. If natural ventilation is required, electromagnetic hold-open devices linked to the fire alarm system must be installed.

What happens if service duct penetrations are left unsealed?

Electrical cable shafts and plumbing ducts that pass through concrete floor slabs act as natural chimneys. Fire in a lower floor basement will draft straight up through unsealed floor gaps, igniting upper levels within minutes. Intumescent firestop pillows expand under heat to create a rock-hard seal.
