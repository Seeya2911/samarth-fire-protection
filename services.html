Fire Safety Services, Equipment & Training in Mumbai

Samarth Fire Protection System is a Class D licensed fire safety agency serving Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane and across Maharashtra. Our services

Fire Protection Services We Offer in Mumbai

Every engagement begins with understanding your property, the fire safety systems already installed and the specific service required. Our priorities are occupant safety, fire safety awareness and customer satisfaction — whether the property is a housing society in Malad, a commercial tower in BKC, or an industrial unit in Thane.

01 · Detection

Fire Alarm & Detection Systems

We supply, install, wire, program, troubleshoot, and maintain automatic addressable and conventional fire alarm panels, smoke/heat detectors, manual call points, and strobe hooters with buzzer fault repairs and AMC contracts.

Explore fire alarm offerings →
02 · Fire fighting

Fire Fighting Systems

We design, erect, test, commission, overhaul, and maintain wet/dry risers, yard hydrants, booster pump automation stations, sprinkler networks, and hose reel drums with comprehensive AMC and emergency repair services.

Explore firefighting offerings →
03 · Review

Fire Safety Audits & Compliance

We conduct on-site physical audits, pump/line pressure testing, and defect gap-analysis, issuing official statutory Form A and bi-annual Form B maintenance compliance certificates under Class D licence MFS/LA/RF-34/RD-35.

Explore audit & compliance offerings →
04 · Equipment

Fire Extinguisher Supply, Refilling & HPT

We supply new ISI-marked ABC, CO₂, Foam, and Clean Agent cylinders, provide scheduled on-site refilling, execute IS 2190 Hydraulic Pressure Testing (HPT), and provide rotational standby loan cylinders during service.

Explore extinguisher offerings →
05 · Passive protection

Passive Fire Protection & Fire Doors

We supply, install, and certify 120-minute Fire Rated Doors (FRD), panic crash hardware, electrical shaft rockwool firestops, intumescent cable coatings, and duct fire barriers to isolate fire and protect exit corridors.

Explore passive fire offerings →
06 · People

Fire Safety, First Aid & CPR Training

We bring training equipment right to your compound for live extinguisher practice (PASS technique), building evacuation mock drills, fire hose drills, and certified First Aid & CPR life-support sessions with formal completion certificates.

Explore training & drill offerings →

From Planning to After-Sales Support

Samarth Fire Protection System handles turnkey fire safety projects that include design, planning, supply, installation, testing, commissioning, maintenance and upgrading of existing fire fighting systems. Every engagement includes quality checks under engineering supervision and after-sales follow-up to keep equipment operating as intended. Scope is defined for each property before work begins.

  • • Design and planning
  • • Supply and installation
  • • Testing and commissioning
  • • AMC and regular servicing
  • • HPT of extinguishers
  • • Repairs and system upgrades
  • • Equipment demonstrations
  • • Training and mock drills

Compliance, NOC & Training Support

We assist with NOC documentation, Form A, Form B and fire safety certification work required by the Directorate of Maharashtra Fire Services. First Aid and CPR training is also available for housing societies, schools and corporate offices. Statutory approvals and certificates are subject to applicable rules, current licence scope, building documents and the competent authority's requirements.
